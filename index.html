Careers in the sky

Lots of people make every flight happen. Here are a few of the jobs, and the school subjects that help.

Pilot

Flies airplanes for airlines, cargo companies, rescue teams or the military.

Helpful subjects: math, physics, geography

Air traffic controller

Talks to pilots by radio and keeps every plane safely spaced apart, in the air and on the ground.

Helpful subjects: math, speaking and listening, geography

Aircraft mechanic

Inspects and fixes engines, wings and landing gear so every plane is safe to fly.

Helpful subjects: shop or tech classes, physics, math

Aerospace engineer

Designs and tests airplanes, drones, rockets and satellites.

Helpful subjects: math, physics, computer science

Electrical engineer

Builds the electronics inside planes: radios, screens, sensors and autopilots.

Helpful subjects: math, physics, robotics, coding

Astronaut

Flies to space. Many astronauts started out as pilots, engineers or scientists.

Helpful subjects: math, science, staying fit

Women who led the way

  • Bessie Coleman was the first African American woman to earn a pilot's license, in 1921. No school in the U.S. would teach her, so she learned to fly in France.
  • Amelia Earhart was the first woman to fly alone across the Atlantic Ocean, in 1932.
  • Katherine Johnson was a NASA mathematician whose math helped send astronauts into orbit and to the Moon.
  • Sally Ride became the first American woman in space in 1983.
  • Mae Jemison became the first African American woman in space in 1992.

Find a discovery flight near you

A discovery flight is a first ride in a small plane with a real pilot. Some programs are free for kids and teens. Ask a parent or guardian to look with you.

EAA Young Eagles

Free first airplane rides for ages 8 to 17, given by volunteer pilots at local airports.

eaa.org

Girls in Aviation Day

A free yearly event by Women in Aviation International, with hands-on aviation fun at events around the world.

wai.org

Civil Air Patrol cadets

For ages 12 to 18: learn about aviation, leadership and science, with chances to fly.

gocivilairpatrol.com
